## Conflicts

None found that CONTRADICT the plan/spec/issue text outright. One
significant **omission** worth flagging prominently even though it is not
a contradiction: the plan/spec text ("`VK_EXT_memory_budget` polling...
`vmaGetHeapBudgets` polling") never mentions the `vmaSetCurrentFrameIndex()`
prerequisite for that polling to actually stay fresh frame-to-frame (row 3)
— without it, the reported budget can silently go stale for up to 30
alloc/free operations, which on a mostly-static scene could mean many
seconds or the entire duration of a level. This is exactly the kind of
gap the gate exists to surface before Task 10 is dispatched.
